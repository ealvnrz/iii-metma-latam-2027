# Institutional logos

The conference organizer confirmed UC Chile as the sole organizing institution and UC Chile, Universidad del Bío-Bío and ANID as supporting institutions. These roles are defined in `src/data/site.ts` and shared by the homepage and committees page.

Assets were retrieved on 7 September 2026. The original artwork is preserved: no recoloring, cropping, stretching, shadows or opacity effects are applied. Logo groups use a white background and retain surrounding clear space. Headings are in English; institutional names and lettering inside official logos retain their original form.

## UC Chile

- File: `uc-chile.svg`.
- Source: the international master-brand library linked from https://www.uc.cl/uso-de-la-marca/.
- Library: https://uccl0.sharepoint.com/:f:/s/uc365_ManualesMarca/Ev4Zq1hPMntAvDT5PX0GLWYBWOge8_1oZqpzkJNte2QAZA?e=45TB2G
- Original: `marca_uc_chile/SVG UC I Chile/UC I Chile_1.svg`.
- Guidance: `manual_marca_uc_chile.pdf` in the same library, pages 8–13. The UC Chile master brand is intended for international contexts. Its crest and wordmark must remain together. Clear space is three times the height of the UC capitals. The layout reserves horizontal margins and vertical space around the complete artwork; the organizing logo receives a larger display size.

## Universidad del Bío-Bío

- File: `ubb.png` (1248 × 823 pixels).
- Original: https://dgce.ubiobio.cl/img/descargas/escudo/d/escudo-color-gradiente.png
- Downloads: https://dgce.ubiobio.cl/descargas.php
- Manual, October 2025: https://dgce.ubiobio.cl/img/manual/Manual_de_Normas_Graficas_UBB2025.pdf
- Guidance: pages 12–18. Use the complete institutional mark (crest and university name), with its original gradient colors on a light background and the protection space shown on page 18. The simplified UBB-only mark is not used for this formal context.

## ANID / Ministry of Science

- File: `anid-minciencia.svg`.
- Original: `LOGOS MINCIENCIA + ANID 2026_COLOR.svg` from https://investigacioneinnovacion.udp.cl/cms/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Logos-2.zip
- Provenance: ANID-authored resources distributed by the Universidad Diego Portales research office at https://investigacioneinnovacion.udp.cl/convocatorias-udp/uso-de-marca-anid-para-proyectos-de-investigacion/.
- Manual 2026: https://investigacioneinnovacion.udp.cl/cms/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Manual-de-marca-ANID-2026.pdf
- Guidance: PDF pages 8–10 specify placement after the other institutional marks and use of the combined Ministry of Science / ANID artwork. ANID therefore remains last in `site.sponsors`, at the right of the desktop row and after the universities when stacked on narrow screens. Preserve the full color asset, its internal spacing and institutional wording.
- Additional project-credit guidance: https://investigacioneinnovacion.udp.cl/cms/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Como-mencionar-a-ANID-en-productos-2026.pdf. If a specific ANID-funded project or competition is credited later, add its confirmed funding acknowledgement separately; no funding instrument or project number is inferred from the sponsor listing.
